# Limitless Hoops Membership Data

## Membership Tiers (Source of Truth Pricing)
Based on the current operational baseline, these are the confirmed pricing structures.

### 1. Recreation
*   **Price:** $225 / month
*   **Billing Frequency:** Monthly
*   **Amenities Included:**
    *   Games
    *   M/W/F Skill Workouts
    *   Sunday Shooting
    *   Saturday Open Gym

### 2. Seasonal
*   **Price:** $200 / month ($600 billed quarterly)
*   **Billing Frequency:** Quarterly
*   **Amenities Included:**
    *   *All Recreation items plus:*
    *   Skill Clinics
    *   Pick-and-Roll Workshops
    *   Winter Camp

### 3. Limitless
*   **Price:** $175 / month ($2,100 billed annually)
*   **Billing Frequency:** Annually
*   **Amenities Included:**
    *   *All Seasonal items plus:*
    *   Summer Camp
    *   Tue/Thu Practices
    *   Combines

### 4. Volunteer
*   **Price:** $200 – $500 (Exact amount varies)
*   **Billing Frequency:** One-time refundable deposit
*   **Rules & Amenities:**
    *   *Includes all Limitless items*
    *   Maximum 4 volunteers per 12-player team.
    *   Requires taking on two staff responsibilities (e.g., Scorekeeping, Photography).
    *   *Note:* Volunteer enrollments do not generate referral/team-building payouts.

## The July Pricing Window (Dynamic Early-Bird Engine)
To support the 2027 Team-Building surge, the system requires a time-based discount engine during the July enrollment window. 
*   **Mechanism:** As July progresses, the price of the annual membership steadily increases each week until it hits the baseline annual rate (e.g., $2,100) by Week 4. 
*   **Goal:** Create urgency and reward families who commit to the newly formed regional teams early.
